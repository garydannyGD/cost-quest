YOUR LEARNING MISSION

Think like a cost accountant.

Identify the purpose of cost information; distinguish cost units and centres; classify direct, indirect and functional costs; calculate prime and production cost; interpret cost behaviour; apply high–low estimation; distinguish fixed, flexible and flexed budgets.

FIELD MANUAL

Explore. Repair. Escape.

  1. Use arrow keys / WASD, or tap a terminal to walk to it. Press Enter near a terminal, or tap it again, to open it.
  2. Choose an answer with the mouse, touch, or keys 1–4. Read the explanation, then Continue.
  3. Repair all four terminals, then walk to the exit. Wrong answers can be retried without losing progress.

MISSION BRIEF

Pocket reference

Prime cost = direct material + direct labour + direct expenses.

Production cost = prime cost + production overheads.

High–low: variable cost per unit = change in total cost ÷ change in activity. Choose highest and lowest activity.

Fixed cost = total cost − (units × variable cost per unit).

Estimated total cost = fixed cost + (units × variable cost per unit).
